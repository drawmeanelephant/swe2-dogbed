SWE-2

September 10, 2026. The first SWE model post-trained on a multi-trillion-parameter base — Kimi K3 under the hood, Cognition's refined RL recipe on top — and the first coding model to sit within a single point of the frontier while costing roughly a third as much to run.

50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main — within one point of Fable 5.1, at 64% lower rollout cost. Within a few points of GPT-6 Astra at roughly a quarter of the price. Ahead of its own predecessor SWE-1.7, Grok 4.6, and GPT-5.6 Sol on the comparison table Cognition published. And 92.8% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — the top of that board.

HOW IT WAS TRAINED

  • Base — Kimi K3: the recipe ported onto open weights at multi-trillion-parameter scale.
  • Recipe — a refinement of the multi-turn RL stack proven on Kevin-32B, SWE-1.5→1.7, scaled to a frontier-size base for the first time.
  • Objective — jointly optimize capability and cost; the Pareto curve is the target, not a single score.
