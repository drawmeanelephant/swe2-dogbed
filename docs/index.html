WHAT IS THIS NODE

This is a museum built out of plain text. Somewhere upstream there is a file called page.knap, a pile of JSON objects, and a single Zig binary named dogbed. The binary chewed the templates, spat out Markdown, pushed it through oliver, and soldered the result into the document you are reading. The entire site — the scanlines, the ticker, the bezel — is one stylesheet and zero lines of JavaScript.

The subject matter deserves the treatment. In under three years, Cognition went from unveiling a demo of “the first AI software engineer” to training reinforcement-learning models at multi-trillion-parameter scale, rescuing an orphaned IDE in a weekend, and shipping a coding model that sits within a point of the frontier at a fraction of the cost. These are the annals of that run.
